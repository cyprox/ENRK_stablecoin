 Roadmap and funding
 Kaspa Dollar is a decentralized dollar on Kaspa. The fundraiser has a target of 5,000,000 KAS and closes on 1 January 2027. If the
 target is not reached by then, every contribution is refunded under the terms at the foot of
 this page. This page sets out what the funds pay for, in what order, and how spending is
 reported.
 Who is building it
 Kaspa Dollar is led by Alexander Safstrom, a Kaspa
 core developer and the author of KIP-16, the zero-knowledge proof verification opcodes for
 Kaspa, with the help and support of the rest of the Kaspa core team.
 January
 Fundraiser closes
 If 5,000,000 KAS has not been reached by 1 January 2027, every contribution is refunded under the terms below.
 January - April
 Build
 The protocol code, the publisher software and the membership covenants, the wallet integrations and the indexer that wallets and explorers need to read vault state.
 May
 Testnet
 Every action runs end to end on testnet: mint, repay, redeem, liquidate, price rounds, publisher ejection, membership changes and settlement. Integrators test against it as a proof of concept, and the publishers run the oracle on testnet for the full month.
 June - August
 Audit and hardening
 Independent review of the code by a third-party firm, a second review of the fixes, and the infrastructure for nodes, indexers and monitoring.
 September
 Mainnet
 Kaspa Dollar goes live. The code is final from this point.
 October onward
 Ecosystem
 Work with other stablecoin providers to strengthen the stablecoin ecosystem on Kaspa, and help integrators bring it to people.
 Use of funds
 The 5,000,000 KAS is divided into two
 allocations: 72% pays for building, representing and promoting the project, and the
 remaining 28% pays for the audit.
 Development, representation and marketing, 72% Independent audit, 28% Development, representation and marketing
 72%: 3,600,000 KAS
 Development fees, relationship building with wallets, exchanges and integrators, travel to
 conferences, promotion and marketing, and representation of Kaspa in discussions with
 partners.
 Independent audit
 28%: 1,400,000 KAS
 A third-party review of the vault and token covenants and the oracle and membership
 covenants; a second review of the fixes; publication of both reports; a public bug bounty
 on the audited code.
 Reporting
 Every transaction out of the fundraising address is published at the end of each month, with
 the amount, the recipient category and the purpose. The audit contracts and both audit
 reports are published in full when they are signed and delivered.
