**kUSD (Task 2a/b).**
- `github.com/Kali123411/kUSD` exists: MIT, created 2026-11-14T17:13:30Z, 8 commits, not a fork.
- README says "TN10 testnet research software. Not audited. Not a product. Not for real value." and "Today all five oracle operator keys are held by the deployer; independent operators are a pre-mainnet gate." Both appear verbatim; `llms.txt` repeats the second.
- `contracts/v2/ag/oracle.ag` implements:
  - 5 operator slots (m0 to m4);
  - median of 3 distinct signed prints;
  - 3% spread (SPREAD_BPS=300);
  - mint price down at once and up at most 10% per hour (MINT_UP_DIV);
  - liquidation price up at once and down at most 25% per hour (LIQ_DOWN_DIV);
  - one hour of rate per refresh (RATE_WINDOW=36000);
  - `freeze` after 36,000 DAA (1 h) and `settle` after 25,920,000 DAA (30 d);
  - `set_member` rotation by 3 other operators;
  - the DAA clock and the no-future-print check.
- Four of the transaction ids in `docs/pitch/evidence/INDEX.md` (oracle genesis, two refreshes, set_member) return `is_accepted: true` on `api-tn10.kaspa.org`. Oracle genesis was accepted at block time 1794673722939. The sheet's claim of 3 accepted transactions is correct, and also understated.
- The repository contains CLAUDE.md and `llms.txt` (an instruction file for an AI assistant), a Rust harness and Argent contracts.

**Chainlink (Task 2c).** The stream name `KAS/USD-RefPrice-mainnet-production` is on the docs page (status live, CexPrice, marketHours Crypto, 16 operators). A testnet twin also exists.

**Pyth (Task 2d).** Hermes `price_feeds?query=kas` returns `Crypto.KAS/USD` (id `dfd3cb51...cab4`, `min_channel fixed_rate@200ms`). The price endpoints (`/v2/updates/price/latest` and `/api/latest_price_feeds`) answer HTTP 401 "unauthorized", as the sheet says.
