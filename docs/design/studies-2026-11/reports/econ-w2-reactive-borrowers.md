## 0. Summary
1. **The W1/V1 headline numbers were partly produced by the exogenous redemption schedule (already shown with the schedule off in W1 section 3 point 3 and table T8; what W2 adds is the behaviour layer).** A3 (fee 3%, junior 30%), KAS -85%, flat index: switching the schedule off (passive owners) moves ENRK p95 13.8 -> 20.0 (paired +6.2, CI [+5.4,+7.2]) and unbacked mean 10.2 -> 15.6. The W1 cell (schedule ON, passive) is reproduced exactly (13.8 / 25.7 / -15.5 / 10.16).
2. **Owner/holder behaviour moves the answer more than any architecture lever, in both directions.** A3 flat85 ENRK p95: 0.2 (very attentive, well-funded owners; an optimistic profile, NOT a proven bound [corrected]) ... 5.6 (central behaviours + holder run) ... 13.3 (central borrower behaviours only) ... 20.0 (passive, no schedule) ... 25.2 (inattentive owners, many speculators/miners). The passive model is neither a conservative nor an optimistic bound: watching/top-up/exit lower losses; speculators and miners raise them; holders redeeming at face lower them again.
3. **Ordering unchanged by behaviour in point estimates (91 of 91 base x profile combinations; ties at zero, and partly by construction: A5 = A3 + S3, A8 = A3 with a larger junior share, unbacked identical for A3 and A8 in 63 of 63 no-run cells) [corrected from "unchanged"/"robust"]:** ENRK p95 ordering A0 >= A3 >= A5 and A3 >= A8 >= STACK; unbacked mean A5 <= A3 and STACK <= A8. S3 (surplus to reserve) always protects junior and the hole more than A3; junior 50% always protects ENRK more than A3 at the price of more kFIAT loss in absolute debt terms (as a share of kFIAT face the loss is LOWER for A8 than A3 in 91/91 cells) [corrected]; with no redemption unbacked is identical for A3 and A8 (junior share only moves the loss between tranches).
4. **A3's junior net per cycle is negative on the three -85% bases in every profile except the friendly one [corrected]; on the full grid it is positive only for EXIT, DEF and CENTRAL_R on flat70 (+1.7, +2.3, +2.9), for DEF on x1.5@70 (+1.5; EXIT and CENTRAL_R are 0.0) and for EXIT on ep2024 (+1.1), and for FRIENDLY_B everywhere (see Review corrections). The "fails h = 10%" result is capped by calm yield r/s = 10%, so it says little about behaviour.** Flat85 values (A3 flat85: -24.9 passive / -14.0 central / -34.8 hostile / +8.6 friendly / -2.6 central+run). A5 gives -2.0 / +2.1 / -10.8 / +10.1 / +7.4.
5. A HYBRID label (ENRK p95 <= 5, unbacked <= 3, latent p95 <= 1); it is NOT a V1 label: V1 SURVIVE = 2 / 1 / 3 (ENRK / latent / unbacked) and HOLD = 5 / 1 [corrected]. Over the 7 adverse bases: A3 and A5 pass 0/7 in passive, central and hostile borrower profiles; STACK passes the ENRK label 7/7 (passive, central) but the unbacked label only 0-1/7; with the holder run A5 and STACK pass all three of the HYBRID label in 5/7 (under the real V1 SURVIVE label: A3 0/7, A5 1/7, STACK 5/7; under HOLD: A5 5/7, A8 7/7, STACK 7/7) [corrected].
6. **Sustainable size is small** (daily liquidator throughput 1% of ADV, not a locked capital stock; unmeasured): debt with unbacked mean <= 3% is 6-7% of ADV (A0/A3/A8) and 12% (A5/STACK) in the passive flat85 cell (16% central); 5% / 8% on x2@85. Demand (T2): a 3% fee leaves 0.48-0.70 of demand and +5 pts withheld surplus removes a further 40-60%.
7. Impact x capacity dominates: A3 flat85 passive ENRK p95 3.0 (impact .04, capacity 10%) to 53.3 (impact .16, capacity 2.5%); behaviour shifts a cell by 5-15 pts. No behaviour profile rescues a thin-market cell.
8. NOT reproduced as an effect: reaction to withheld surplus (a property of the model gating, not evidence that withheld surplus leaves owner behaviour unchanged; W2-15). Raising the self-liquidation trigger when surplus is withheld (bump 0/.1/.2/.3) leaves A5 ENRK p95 at 3.7 (flat85) because attention, delay and hazard gates bind, not the ICR trigger. The T2 demand semi-elasticity is used only in the demand table, not fed back into the crash.

## 7. Findings by weight
Robust inside the model: (a) the architecture ordering is unchanged by behaviour in point estimates (91 of 91 base x profile combinations; 7 tied zeros; partly by construction, see summary point 3); (b) the hole does not vanish in non-friendly profiles on adverse bases (A3 flat85 unbacked 15.6 / 10.0 / 20.7 passive / central / hostile; x2@85 26.8 / 17.2 / 32.9; only FRIENDLY_B 0.7-0.9 and the central holder run 4.7-8.1 go lower); (c) no owner action helps a vault that jumps from healthy to underwater in one day, hence the knife-edge latent hole (1.6-8.4% of paths) in every profile. Depends on unanchored assumptions: every headline level; the W1 passive and the "central" values coincide by accident (13.8 vs 13.3).
Proposals / findings for the ledger (not decisions):
- P-A. Earlier ENRK p95 quoted without a redemption mechanism were conditional on the schedule; without it A3/A5 are 19-20 (flat85) and 25-26 (x2@85).
- P-B. The holder run is the biggest lever (ENRK p95 -9.5 on A3 flat85) and the least verified: it needs ENRK below face, arbitrage capital and redemption against lowest-ICR vaults first, which V4/P9 says L1 can only encourage by price, not guarantee. A design relying on it should guarantee order or not count on it.
- P-C. Owner attention and delay are worth a lot (attention 0 -> 1: 9.8 -> 3.9; delay 14 d -> 0: 11.3 -> 4.0): anything shortening reaction time (alerts, a visible ICR, a one-transaction top-up/close) is a modelled lever; whether owners use it is unmeasured. Test idea, not a recommendation.
- P-D. The cost of self-liquidation falls on the KAS market (0.5-0.8 days of ADV in central cells); impact and capacity dominate every behaviour.
- P-E. S3 protects junior and the hole in every profile but reduces demand; the "bump" reaction is not visible, so no claim that S3 pushes owners into pre-emptive exits.

## 8. Not modelled / limitations (unverified points)
Behaviour parameters are my ranges, not empirical. Top-ups do not support the KAS price; repayments are at face (ENRK at par); self-liquidation executes at the day's price minus a haircut and the impact is applied at the end of the day. Speculators are static, re-levered at pre-year start; speculators and miners never act. Miner model is a collateral drain with guessed share/opex, no hash-rate exit. Demand response is analytic and not fed back; composition effects (who leaves first) are not modelled. Fee reaction inside the crash is not modelled; fee > 3% untested (153% cliff). The holder run assumes redemption at face with arbitrage capital; no ENRK secondary price is simulated; a run by selling on the market only is not covered. Oracle lag/wrong price, multi-event crashes, correlated liquidator capital: not modelled (V5 blind spots 2, 3, 7). OAT/SENS/SIZE have 300 paths (more sampling noise than the 500-path HL); size ceilings use 6 grid points, linear interpolation, grid ends at 1.0 x base. ENRK p95 differences below 1 point (STACK 0.0 when over 95% of paths have no write-off) should not be read. An independent review was done on 2026-11-14 (review-w1-x1b-w2-independent.md): numbers reproduce, no code bug found; coverage gaps in check_w2 are listed in Review corrections W2-8. A first launch of the Monte Carlo chain via nohup was killed with the shell and relaunched; no result mixes the two attempts.
