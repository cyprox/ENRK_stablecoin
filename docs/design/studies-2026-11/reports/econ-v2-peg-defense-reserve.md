## 1. Model (peg_model.py, transparent daily model)
p = anchor - I/D (I = net sell overhang, D = depth). Overhang decays at kappa/day. Redemption arbitrage below peg (fee 1%, friction c, lowest-ICR-first only to eligible vaults, capital Ka recycled in 2 d), mint ceiling above (2%), yield-chasing carry capital Kc, reserve R fed by g = 3/6/12% of debt per year (econ-m1 range). Liquidity shock: 10/20/30% of supply sold over 2 days, collateral intact. Solvency shock: absolute shortfall l = 2/3/10/14/20/26% of debt (econ-t3: orig85 mean 2.07, orig85 ENRK p95 3.0, flat85 mean 10.2 / p95 13.8, x2 19.6, x3 26.5); it stays on the remaining ENRK (par redemption of eligible vaults), eligible share = 1 - l/0.29 (calibrated on ONE figure: 26% of redemption demand has no vault at 7.5% loss, FROZEN s5/s7), panic selling, partial recovery rec 0/0.5.
Policies: a none; b rebate of the redemption fee; c yield on locked ENRK = f(reserve ratio); d yield = f(peg deviation) with trigger ORA (ENRK oracle: noise 0.3-5%, delay 0-3 d, manipulation) or RV (7-day redemption volume, no price); e free/paid debt split + exponential controller (half-life 10/30/90 d, floor 0.5%, explicit max rate 10%-100% or none, borrower switching elasticity eta, demand semi-elasticity 3/10/20% per pt from econ-t2); e0 same split with fixed rate; f reserve absorbs loss pro rata (no yield), with optional ring-fence nu.
ASSUMED, load-bearing (all swept): locked ENRK cannot sell in a shock (shield 1/0.5/0), carry capital Kc 3/10/30% of supply, arb capital Ka 2/5/15%, D 1/2/4, c 0.25-1%, lock response, switching speed, i.i.d. oracle noise. In (e) only free debt is redeemable (Monolith rule); in a..d all debt with ICR>=100%.

## 4. Oracle quality and attackers (E3, E6, E7)
Own-oracle noise (i.i.d., no manipulation, quiet market): (d, y50, th 2%) pays 0.000 / 0.008 / 0.89 / 2.5 pts of debt in 600 d at noise 0.3 / 1 / 3 / 5%; share of payout made while the true price is within 1 pt of peg: 78-87% at 3-5% noise; delay of 0-3 d changes nothing material. Lower threshold (1%) at noise 1% pays 0.18 pts (y50). (b) leaks 0.05 pts and triggers redemptions at noise (redeemed 4.7-5.9% of supply in a quiet market vs 0.5% without it: lowest-ICR borrowers are redeemed on noise). So a noisy oracle turns the policy into a subsidy to lockers/redeemers (reserve outflow with no depeg).
Manipulation (observed price pushed down by delta for T days, no true depeg), (d,y50): outflow 0.06 pts (6%, 5 d) to 0.62 pts (19% of the reserve at that date, 20 d); y100: 1.4 pts (42%). 70-90% goes to ordinary lockers, not the attacker (dilution). Attacker with A=5% pre-locked is profitable only if the oracle venue is shallow: break-even Dv* (supply fraction that moves the venue price 100%; cost/day = 0.5 delta^2 Dv, ASSUMED) is 0.025 (y50) to 0.06 (y100) at best delta 5-6%; A=1%: 0.005-0.012; A=20%: 0.10-0.24. (b): manipulation changes nothing (no arbitrage at true peg). (c) and (e) have no oracle input.
Wash trading: volume trigger (d_RV): payout/wash cost 0.07-2.2 and net negative for a 5% locker in all 6 cells (wash cost 3% of volume). Flash holders without a minimum lock multiply outflow 7x-20x (d, quiet) and 1.1-1.6x (c); a 7-day minimum lock removes it (E7). Timing: pre-locking before a known depeg earns the y path on its whole principal (d: yield 2.7% of principal in a 20-d depeg at y50 vs 1.2% opportunity cost for 30 d at 15%/yr, analytic): positive for insiders; not run as a separate sim. Free-ratio manipulation of (e) (shift 5-20% for 20-60 d): max rate 4.0-7.5%, extra interest 0.14-0.30 pts of debt vs attacker cost 0.01-0.15 pts: grief, no extraction.

## 5. Monolith controller (E4, E9)
In peg events (liquidity 20%, sustained drip, E4: 135 controller cells per scenario) the rate rises only to a mean maximum of 4-6% (p95 6-8%) and the explicit cap never binds (cap values 10%-none give identical rows): borrowers switch to free debt before the controller runs. It adds nothing to the peg: vs fixed-rate split area +0 to +3% (liquidity), negative vs "all debt redeemable" (-8 to -15%, because only free debt is redeemable: structural loss of the redemption floor). Cost: extra interest 0.4-0.6 pts of debt over 2 y, debt contraction to 0.88-0.94.
Where the cap matters: when free ratio is depressed by something that is not the peg (E9, preference shift -0.4/-0.8 of log target, f=0): with eta 0-0.05 the uncapped rate reaches a median 10-16% at day 360 and 22-29% at day 720 (p90 37-65%, mean max 25-38%), debt falls to 19-33% of its level; a 10% cap holds the rate at 10% and debt at 41-46% (still a large loss). With eta >= 0.15 the rate stays 4-9% and the cap is irrelevant. Closed form: 3% doubling every 30 d reaches 50% in 122 d (hence a cap is needed in the stuck case, 13,793%/yr uncapped after a year). Opposite shift (+0.8): rate sits at the 0.5% floor, debt +33% (mint expansion). Oscillation: 0.5-3 direction flips per year; more with preference noise sig_u 0.30 (2.5-3.3/yr, sd log r 0.3-0.46) and longer half-life lowers it (flips 0.9 at 90 d vs 1.5 at 10 d) at the price of a slower response (median r at day 360: 15.8 vs 9.8 at hl 10 vs 90). The signal is contaminated: free ratio measures borrower preference as much as peg pressure.

## 6. Verdicts
P6 (owner idea: fee reserve granting depeg-triggered yield): PARTLY CONFIRMED / NEEDS DECISION. Confirmed for small, transient liquidity depegs only in the cheapest form, the redemption-fee rebate (b), which is a fee cut on demand, not a yield (+41%, 0.17 pts, but harms when the cause is insolvency and leaks on noise). The depeg-triggered yield (d) is REFUTED as a peg tool in this model for 2-day shocks (+5-12%, higher with large carry capital and thin arbitrage), REFUTED for solvency shocks (0 benefit, burns the reserve), and it creates an oracle-error subsidy above 2-3% oracle noise and a manipulation payout (up to 19-44% of the reserve in 20 d) unless the venue is deep; flash accrual needs a minimum lock. Reserve-ratio yield (c) works (+64-71%) but only by paying continuously (spends 4-8 pts, halving the loss-absorbing reserve) and its effect halves if locked holders can sell. Loss absorption (f) dominates for solvency and costs no oracle; it is also the only variant compatible with FROZEN s7 (pro rata) but needs global state (not available on L1). Whether to spend on incentives or on absorption is the owner's call: break-even in the model is that every pt of reserve spent on yield reduces absorbable loss pt for pt.
P7 (free/paid split with rate controller, explicit max rate): PARTLY CONFIRMED. The oracle-free signal works mechanically (rate responds to redemptions) but is too weak and too slow to defend the peg in the model, and it removes redeemability from paid debt (the redemption floor shrinks to the free share). An explicit max rate is CONFIRMED as necessary: without it a stuck ratio gives 25-65% rates and 70-80% debt loss in 2 y; with it the loss is bounded but still large. Conditions: harmless if eta >= 0.15 (borrowers move to free quickly), harmful if eta < 0.05 or the ratio is driven by non-peg preferences.
P8 trigger question: need an ENRK market price: (d)-ORA, (b)-ORA, and the oracle part of any policy that must act BEFORE redemptions occur. Do not need it: (c) (reserve ratio), (f), (e) (free ratio), (b)/(d) with redemption-volume trigger (works only once the price is below fee+friction, i.e. ~98.5%, and costs more per point: 0.25 vs 0.03), minimum-lock yields. Redemption volume and free ratio are manipulable only at a cost of 3% of volume (negative net in all 6 wash cells) or by own-debt mode switching (grief only).
