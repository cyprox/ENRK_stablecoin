RECOVERY MODE vs STATIC PARAMETERISATION
120 Monte Carlo paths per configuration, 1000 vaults, opening ICR 200%
latent = unbacked debt not yet realised, the freeze metric
discount = value transferred from vault owners to liquidators

3. Protection bought, against cost paid
=======================================
Improvement is the reduction in the p95 latent hole under crash.
Cost is extra liquidations and extra discount paid in calm markets.

configuration                           latent p95  calm liq  calm disc
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
baseline  MCR 150 / floor 85                   +0%        +0      +0.0%
RECOVERY MODE  trigger 150 -> MCR 200          +0%        -0      +0.0%
static  MCR 175 / floor 85                     -0%      +214      +3.8%
static  MCR 200 / floor 85                     -1%      +280      +5.2%
static  MCR 150 / floor 75                    -46%      +136      +2.4%
static  MCR 150 / floor 70                    -46%      +136      +2.4%
static  MCR 150 / floor 60                    -46%      +136      +2.4%
static  MCR 175 / floor 70                    -46%      +292      +4.6%

Negative latent = better protection. Positive costs = worse in calm markets.

The row to compare everything against is RECOVERY MODE: it is what
Igra EVM can do and Kaspa L1 cannot. Any static configuration that
matches its protection while costing little in calm conditions is a
viable substitute; one that only matches it by liquidating people in
ordinary markets is not.
