6. Where the Dutch auction stops clearing
=========================================
The auction floor is 85% of market, a 15% discount. Liquidators
demand base 4% + 18% x drawdown. Beyond the crossover, no auction
fills at any point in the descent:
    required = 4% + 18% x drawdown  >  15%  when  drawdown > 61%
